About Yuanno Digital

We turn paper processes into digital workflows that actually work.

Yuanno Digital helps local businesses with digitalization, automation, and optimization. We keep things simple and human, so your tools feel like a helping hand and not another headache.

Our goals

We aim to be the best technological help there is for local businesses: clear, reliable and easy to reach.

Digitalization

Start small: a digital menu, a simple booking form, a cleaner website. We focus on quick wins that make your daily work easier.

Automation

Stop copying and pasting the same information everywhere. We connect your tools so basic tasks run automatically in the background.

Optimization

We keep adjusting and improving. Looking at data, listening to you and making sure your digital tools actually bring results.

Who?

Founders

Yuanno Digital is built by people who grew up between notebooks and screens. We understand small businesses that still work a lot on paper, but also know exactly how digital tools can help them grow.

Our approach

How we like to work with the businesses that trust us.

Down to earth

We speak normal language, not tech jargon. We visit your shop, sit with you, and design solutions that match how you actually work.

Transparent

Clear prices, clear timelines, clear expectations. You always know what we are doing and why – no hidden costs or vague promises.

Growth-oriented

Every project is built so you can grow: more clients, better visibility and more time to focus on what you do best.

Working with businesses near and far

We’re starting from our base in Belgium, but we think and work internationally. Most projects can be done fully online, with clear communication and simple tools that you and your team can use from anywhere.

Whether you are a small bakery, a café, a local shop or a growing online business, we shape our services around your reality – not the other way around.

Where are we based?

Yuanno Digital is currently based in Belgium, with a heart in Brussels.
