Services

Practical help,
step by step.

Dubsar helps you understand letters, fill in forms and organise your documents. We work through what needs to be done together, while you stay in control of the next steps.

Understand a letter

We read your letter with you to understand what is being asked and work out the next steps.

For exampleLetters, requests for information and administrative documents.

Fill in a form

We help you gather the right information and complete your form, step by step.

For examplePaper or online forms and preparing supporting documents.

Organise your documents

We bring your documents together, check what is missing and help you get everything in order.

For exampleSorting paperwork, making a checklist and preparing an application.

Follow up on a request

We help you keep track of deadlines and prepare questions or replies about your application.

For exampleImportant dates, progress enquiries and preparing for an appointment.

Practical help, with clear expectations.

We help you understand and prepare your paperwork. The organisations handling your request make their own decisions. If your question needs a specialist, we will let you know.

A few questions, simply answered.

Which languages can you help me in?+

We offer support in French, Dutch and English. Choose the language you feel most comfortable with.

What should I prepare before getting in touch?+

Simply describe what you need help with. We can then work out which documents are useful. Please don’t send sensitive documents with your first message.

How much does the help cost?+

We agree on the approach and price before starting, depending on your request. Get in touch to discuss what you need.

Can you guarantee the outcome of my application?+

We help you prepare and follow up on your paperwork. We cannot guarantee a decision or response time from an administration.

Worried about your paperwork?

You don’t need to have all the answers before getting in touch. Let’s start with your question.
