Here is how this website works and what to share when you first get in touch.

A simple website

This website has no analytics tools, advertising, data collection forms or application cookies. Fonts and graphics are served with the site. The hosting provider may process technical connection data needed to operate the website.

When you contact us

If you call or email us, you share the information you choose to provide. Keep your first message to a description of your question and the contact details we need to reply.

Sensitive documents

Please don’t send identity documents or other sensitive documents with your first message. If documents are needed, we will agree with you on how to share them.
