Here is how this website works and what to share when you first get in touch.

How the website works

This website has no advertising or data collection forms. Fonts and graphics are served with the site. The hosting provider may process technical connection data needed to operate the website. Google Analytics is optional and stays disabled until you accept it.

Visitor statistics and your choice

With your permission, Google Analytics helps us understand visit numbers and how pages are used. Google receives information about pages visited, your device and browser, and technical connection data, including your IP address. Our configuration does not send form or email content. Advertising features and personalisation are disabled.

You can accept or refuse this measurement. No requests are sent to Google and no analytics cookies are set before you accept. Your choice is kept in your browser’s local storage for 180 days. Analytics cookies last no more than 180 days. Our page-view events use page addresses without query parameters or fragments.

You can change your choice or withdraw your permission at any time using “Cookie preferences” in the footer. Refusing stops measurement and removes the Google Analytics cookies set by this website. It does not retrospectively remove data already processed. Read Google’s privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you call or email us, you share the information you choose to provide. Keep your first message to a description of your question and the contact details we need to reply.

Sensitive documents

Please don’t send identity documents or other sensitive documents with your first message. If documents are needed, we will agree with you on how to share them.
